The **Community Presets Hub** is a marketplace where Figranium users can share and discover automation workflows.

## Overview

The Hub allows you to:

- **Browse Presets**: Discover tasks and workspace configurations created by the community.
- **Import Workflows**: Download and import presets directly into your Figranium instance.
- **Publish Presets**: Share your own battle-tested workflows with other users.

## How to Use

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access the Hub">
    Navigate to the **Presets** section in the Figranium dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter and search">
    Find presets based on tags, popularity, or specific use cases (e.g., "E-commerce", "Social Media").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import a preset">
    Once you find a preset you like, download it and click **Import** to add it to your tasks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize">
    After importing, you can modify the task to suit your specific needs (e.g., updating selectors or variables).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Contributing to the Hub

If you've built a workflow that solves a common problem, consider sharing it!

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export your task">
    Use the **Export** feature in the Task Editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit to the Hub">
    Upload the exported JSON file along with a description and expected output.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Always review imported tasks and their JavaScript blocks before running them against sensitive sites.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Start with a highly-rated preset that matches your use case, then customize selectors and variables for your target site rather than building from scratch.
</Tip>
