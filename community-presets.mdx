The **Community Presets Hub** is a marketplace where Figranium users can share and discover automation workflows.

## **Overview**

The Hub allows you to:

- **Browse Presets**: Discover tasks and workspace configurations created by the community.
- **Import Workflows**: Download and import presets directly into your Figranium instance.
- **Publish Presets**: Share your own battle-tested workflows with other users.

## **How to Use**

1. **Access the Hub**: Navigate to the **Presets** section in the Figranium dashboard.
2. **Filter & Search**: Find presets based on tags, popularity, or specific use cases (e.g., "E-commerce", "Social Media").
3. **One-Click Import**: Once you find a preset you like,  download it and click **Import** to add it to your tasks.
4. **Customize**: After importing, you can modify the task to suit your specific needs (e.g., updating selectors or variables).

## **Contributing to the Hub**

If you've built a workflow that solves a common problem, consider sharing it!

- **Export Task**: Use the **Export** feature in the Task Editor.
- **Submit to Hub**: Upload the exported JSON file along with a description and expected output.

## **Safety & Quality**

Always review imported tasks and their JavaScript blocks before running them against sensitive sites.
