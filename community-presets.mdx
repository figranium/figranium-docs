The **Templates** screen lets you browse ready-to-use community automations from the official [Figranium Templates Hub](https://templates.figranium.dev) and import them straight into your workspace. Use a template when you want a working starting point for a common job, such as monitoring a page, collecting leads, or testing a flow, instead of building every action from scratch.

## Overview

From the Templates screen you can:

- **Browse templates**: Discover automations created by the community, grouped by category.
- **Preview before importing**: Read each template's README and see the output it is expected to produce.
- **Import in one click**: Add a template to your workspace as a new task and open it in the editor.
- **Publish templates**: Share your own battle-tested workflows with other users through the Hub.

## Browse and import a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Templates">
    Click **Templates** in the sidebar, or press <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>2</kbd> (<kbd>Option</kbd> + <kbd>2</kbd> on macOS).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search, filter, and sort">
    Type in the **Search templates, sites, or use cases…** field to match titles, descriptions, creator names, categories, and target URLs. Use the category filter to narrow results to **Developer Tools**, **Lead Gen**, **Monitoring**, **Other**, **QA Testing**, **Shopping**, or **Social Media**, and sort by **Most popular**, **Newest**, or **Name**. Click **Load more templates** to see more results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview a template">
    Click a template card to open its preview. The preview shows the creator, the template's README, and its expected output. Cards and previews show a verified badge next to verified creators.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import the template">
    Click **Import** on the card or in the preview. Figranium adds the template to your workspace as a new task and opens it in the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize">
    Adjust the imported task to fit your needs, for example by updating selectors, variables, or the target URL.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Start from the dashboard

You can also reach templates from the dashboard:

- **Create Task menu**: Open the arrow menu next to the **Create Task** button and choose **Create from a template** to go to the Templates screen, or **Start from scratch** to open a blank task.
- **Featured templates**: When your workspace has no tasks yet, the dashboard shows **Start with a popular template** with the three most popular templates and an option to build your own.

<Note>
  Figranium loads the catalog from the Templates Hub and caches it for 5 minutes. If the Hub can't be reached and no cached copy is available, the Templates screen shows **Templates are unavailable** with a **Retry** button.
</Note>

## Template API

Signed-in workspace sessions can list, search, and fetch templates through the REST API. See [Templates](/rest-api#templates) in the REST API reference.

## Contributing to the Hub

If you've built a workflow that solves a common problem, consider sharing it!

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export your task">
    Use the **Export** feature in the Task Editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit to the Hub">
    Go to the [Templates Hub](https://templates.figranium.dev) and upload the exported JSON file along with a description and expected output.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Always review imported tasks and their JavaScript blocks before running them against sensitive sites.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Start with a popular template that matches your use case, then customize selectors and variables for your target site rather than building from scratch.
</Tip>
