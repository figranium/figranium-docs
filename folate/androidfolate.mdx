<Info>
AndroidFolate is planned for **January 2027**. Weights, bindings, and benchmark results are not published yet. Details below may change.
</Info>

AndroidFolate brings the Folate approach to Android. It ranks interactive nodes in the live accessibility hierarchy for single-step actions (`click`, `type`, `scroll`, `long_click`), returning only nodes that actually exist.

## Planned model

| Artifact | Backbone | Format | Size | Runtime |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `model_quantized.tflite` | `google/electra-large-discriminator` (335M) | Static INT8 LiteRT | ~335 MB | NPU delegates and desktop host |
| `androidfolate-large` | `google/electra-large-discriminator` | `float32` Safetensors | ~1.34 GB | PyTorch / training |

## Supported UI frameworks

Native Android Views and Jetpack Compose, React Native, Flutter Semantics trees, and embedded WebViews share one serialization schema.

## Hardware

**Physical devices** need a transformer-capable NPU and at least 6 GB of RAM:

- **6 GB (minimum)**: the NPU session unloads after inference to avoid memory pressure.
- **8 GB or more (recommended)**: the session stays warm for zero cold-start latency.

| NPU vendor | Supported SoCs |
| :--- | :--- |
| Google Tensor | Tensor G2 and newer (Pixel 7 series onward) |
| Qualcomm Hexagon | Snapdragon 8-series and upper 7-series |
| Samsung Exynos | Exynos 1380, 1480, 1580, and 2400+ |
| MediaTek APU | Dimensity 7200, 8000-series, and 9000-series |

Devices with 4 GB of RAM or less, and SoCs without a transformer-capable NPU, are not supported.

**Emulators** (Android Studio AVD, Cuttlefish, Waydroid, Genymotion, and desktop Android VMs) are first-class. AndroidFolate sends the accessibility tree over a local ADB or gRPC bridge and runs inference on the host: Apple Neural Engine, desktop GPU, or CPU XNNPACK.
