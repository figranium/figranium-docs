This guide outlines the formal phonetic standards, International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) transcriptions, syllabification, and acceptable spoken variants for **Figranium**.

## **Phonetic Standards**

The name **Figranium** is pronounced as a four-syllable word with primary stress on the second syllable (`-gra-`).

Two standard phonetic pronunciations are formally recognized:

| Variant | IPA Transcription | Respelling | Phonetic Breakdown |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard (Short i)** | `/fɪˈɡræniəm/` | **fig-RAN-ee-um** or **phi-GRAN-ee-um** | `/fɪ/` (as in *fig*) + `/ˈɡræn/` (as in *granite*) + `/i.əm/` (as in *titanium*) |
| **Alternate (Long i)** | `/faɪˈɡræniəm/` | **fy-GRAN-ee-um** | `/faɪ/` (as in *fly*) + `/ˈɡræn/` (as in *granite*) + `/i.əm/` (as in *titanium*) |

---

## **Syllable & Stress Analysis**

- **Syllabification**: `Fi · gra · ni · um` (4 syllables)
- **Primary Stress**: Syllable 2 (`/ˈɡræn/`)
- **Secondary / Initial Syllable**:
  - `/fɪ/` (Near-close near-front unrounded vowel) OR
  - `/faɪ/` (Open front unrounded vowel to near-close near-front unrounded vowel diphthong)
- **Terminal Suffix**: `/i.əm/` (Unstressed Latinate suffix, common in elemental names)

---

## **Phonetic Components**

1. **Initial Syllable (`Fi-`)**:
   - `/fɪ/` ("Phi-", rhyming with *bit* or *fig*)
   - `/faɪ/` ("Fy-", rhyming with *sky* or *fly*)
2. **Medial Syllable (`-gran-`)**:
   - `/ˈɡræn/` (Rhyming with *plan* or *can*, containing the primary stress)
3. **Final Syllables (`-ium`)**:
   - `/ni.əm/` (Two short unstressed syllables, sounding like "nee-um")

---

## **Summary**

Both **"Phi-granium"** (`/fɪˈɡræniəm/`) and **"Fy-granium"** (`/faɪˈɡræniəm/`) are formally acceptable pronunciations within technical communications and community presentations.
