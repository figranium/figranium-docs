This guide outlines the official pronunciation for **Figranium**.

## **Official Pronunciation**

The name **Figranium** is pronounced as a four-syllable word:

| Syllable | Pronunciation Guide |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Phy-** | Rhymes with *hi* or *tie* |
| **-gray-** | Rhymes with *ray* |
| **-nee-** | Rhymes with *knee* |
| **-um** | Rhymes with *stadium* |

---

## **Summary**

**Phy-gray-nee-um** is the only correct way to pronounce Figranium.
