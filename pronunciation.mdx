This guide outlines the single official pronunciation standard for **Figranium**.

## **Official Pronunciation**

The name **Figranium** is pronounced exclusively as **Phy-gray-nee-um**.

**IPA Pronunciation:** `/faɪˈɡreɪ.ni.əm/`

### **Syllable Breakdown**

- **Phy-** (rhymes with _hi_ or _tie_)
- **-gray-** (rhymes with _ray_)
- **-nee-** (rhymes with _knee_)
- **-um** (rhymes with _stadium_)

---

## **Etymology and Phonetics**

While conventional reading rules might suggest pronouncing the prefix with a short "fi" (`/fɪ/`), the original concept for the name intended a long "fi" (`/faɪ/`). The long "fi" sound was chosen because it feels catchier and less robotic.

---

## **Summary**

**Phy-gray-nee-um** (`/faɪˈɡreɪ.ni.əm/`) is the official and recognized pronunciation for Figranium across all official documentation, communications, and community presentations.
